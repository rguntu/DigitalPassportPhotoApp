# Digital Passport Photo App Features

## Flow
1. Choose passport **country** (US, UK, CA, AU, DE) with size hint (e.g. US 2×2 @ 300 DPI)
2. **Take** or **Upload** a photo (camera oval guide; upload does not need camera permission)
3. **Adjust** — step indicator, optional BG removal, Before/After, compliance checklist, Save
4. Gallery: **Edit**, free **Preview** (2, watermarked), or **Print sheet ($1)** (6)
5. After purchase: clean **Share** / **Print** 4×6 sheet

## UX details
- Shared theme (`#1d9bf0` / `#F0F5F9`), buttons, and Capture → Adjust → Review → Print steps
- Print/share cell aspect follows the selected country’s passport dimensions
- Disclaimers: not a government service; acceptance not guaranteed
- Consumable IAP unlocks one clean sheet; free preview stays watermarked
